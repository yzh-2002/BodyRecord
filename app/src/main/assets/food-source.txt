USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy release (April 2018).
Source: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/
License: public domain / CC0, https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/

The app bundles a curated subset of 27 generic foods for offline reference.
Values are grams of carbohydrate by difference (nutrient 1005), protein
(1003), and total fat (1004), per 100 grams edible portion, preserving the
source's weighed/prepared state. The Chinese labels and explanatory text
are app-authored. Exact FDC IDs, source names, and archive checksum are
recorded in docs/food-source-manifest.json.

The body-weight intake reference is separately transcribed from the user's
provided image. It is a coefficient display and multiplication tool, not a
nutritional-needs estimate, calorie target, or automatic diet adjustment.
